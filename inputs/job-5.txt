But you're also excited about how AI is changing software development. You use tools like Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and emerging coding agents to accelerate implementation, explore unfamiliar codebases, write tests, troubleshoot problems, and automate development workflows.

You're primarily a backend engineer, but you're comfortable moving into React, TypeScript, and Next.js to build complete features and deliver working applications. You don't need a frontend specialist for every screen, form, or dashboard.

We're looking for someone who combines the technical depth of an experienced .NET engineer, strong Azure cloud engineering skills, full-stack versatility, and an AI-native approach to building software.
